# Yearly overview

Status: idea.

## What it does

Achievements for biking, such as a first 1,000 km in a year or a month
without a missed day. The totals for this year are already part of the
[App](../roadmap/app.md).

## Why it is interesting

Seeing what biking adds up to is motivating.

## What it would touch

The app's main screen.

## Open questions

- Which achievements, and when they unlock.
- Where they are shown.
