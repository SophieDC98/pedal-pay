# Yearly overview

Status: idea.

## What it does

An overview with achievements and totals, such as km biked this year and money earned from the allowance.

## Why it is interesting

Seeing what biking adds up to is motivating, and the money makes the allowance tangible.

## What it would touch

The app, which would need the allowance rate per km.

## Open questions

- Where the rate per km comes from, and how it changes each year.
- Which achievements, and when they unlock.
