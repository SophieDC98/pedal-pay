# Claimed months

Status: idea.

## What it does

The app remembers which months were filled into ERPx, marks them in the
calendar, and warns before the fill bookmark claims the same month twice.

## Why it is interesting

Claims are made monthly; a mark shows at a glance what is still to do.

## What it would touch

Storage, the app's calendar and the fill bookmark, which would have to tell
the app what it created.

## Open questions

- Whether a month counts as claimed when only some of its rides were added.
